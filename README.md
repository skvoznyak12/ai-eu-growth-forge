# EU AI Ascent

Act as a Lead Full-Stack Web Developer and UX/UI Designer. Your task is to design and write the complete, production-ready frontend code (HTML5, Tailwind CSS, clean JavaScript) for a modern, high-converting B2B website for an AI Marketing & Content Studio targeting European enterprise clients.

Business Context & Positioning:

Core Offer: Help businesses scale marketing, produce high-volume content, and acquire clients in Belgium, Switzerland, and the broader EU market using AI, video production, and performance marketing.

Key Positioning: We are a high-performance European B2B digital agency leveraging AI as an efficiency tool (Technology + Creativity + Performance + EU Market Expertise), NOT an experimental AI toy.

Target Audience: CEOs, CMOs, Marketing Directors, SMEs, Tech Startups, E-commerce, and B2B services in the EU.

Primary Conversion Goal (CTA): "Book a Consultation" / "Request a Proposal".

Design & Technical Requirements:

Design System: Sleek, trustworthy European agency aesthetic inspired by Jellyfish, DEPT®, and Monks. Clean dark/light hybrid theme, crisp typography, smooth micro-interactions, responsive design (mobile/tablet/desktop).

Language: English (Default) with an interactive UI toggle for French (FR) and German (DE).

GDPR & EU Compliance: Include a functional Cookie Consent banner with granular settings, explicit form-level data consent checkboxes, and clear privacy disclaimers.

Interactive Logic: Mobile menu, smooth scrolling, functional tab filters (for services/cases), interactive contact form with lead-routing simulation (showing how submissions process to contact@agency.com), and modal windows for booking.

Site Architecture & Required Sections:

Header / Navigation:

Logo, Navigation links to main sections, Language Switcher (EN | FR | DE), Primary CTA ("Book a Consultation").

Home Page (Hero & Overview):

Hero: High-impact value proposition ("Scale Your Marketing Across Europe with AI Speed & Human Creativity"), primary and secondary CTAs, social proof/metrics bar (+300% ROI, 5+ EU Markets).

Value Proposition & Workflow: Interactive diagram showing AI + Human Video Production + Performance pipeline.

Featured Work Preview: Quick-grid of top 3 cases.

Trust Factors: Client logos, video testimonials, interactive ROI calculator.

Services Section (Detailed Breakdown):

Tabbed or grid layout covering:

Turnkey YouTube Channels & Video Content

AI-Driven Text, Image & Video Generation

Professional Video Production & Editing

Digital Advertising & Performance Marketing

SEO & Content Marketing

Multilingual Content & Localization (EU Focus)

EU Market Brand Scaling

Cases & Results (Portfolio):

Filterable portfolio grid (by Industry: B2B, SaaS, E-commerce; and Service type).

Detailed case cards featuring: Problem, AI + Human Solution, Quantifiable EU Results (e.g., "+150% Leads in Belgium & Switzerland").

About Us:

Agency philosophy ("AI Efficiency Meets European Quality"), leadership team, dual-office presence (Brussels/Zurich concept), core stack & technology partners.

Blog / Insights:

Article grid focused on EU marketing trends, AI video scaling, and GDPR-compliant marketing strategies. Includes newsletter opt-in with GDPR consent.

Careers:

"Join Our Hybrid Team" section with dynamic job filters (AI Prompt Engineer, Senior Performance Marketer, EU Video Editor) and instant application modal.

Investors / Company Growth:

Strategic growth roadmap, performance metrics, downloadable "Investor One-Pager", and dedicated IR contact trigger.

Contacts & Lead Routing:

Interactive form: Name, Corporate Email, Company, Budget, Target Markets (EU checkboxes), Message, GDPR consent checkbox.

Simulated Lead Flow UI: On form submission, display a live visual feedback showing: Submission Received -> GDPR Validated -> Routed to CRM -> Assigned to Account Director (brussels@studio.com).

Office addresses, corporate email (hello@studio.com), and calendar booking widget placeholder.

Footer & GDPR Elements:

Footer links to Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Terms, Imprint (Impressum).

Persistent Cookie Consent banner at the bottom with "Accept All", "Reject Non-Essential", and "Manage Preferences".

Deliverables:
Provide complete, beautifully formatted HTML/Tailwind CSS code with inline JS for interactive elements, ready to be previewed directly in a browser. Ensure all copy is written in natural, professional B2B English.
